7 · Snap-fit covers

How the catches work

  • Print direction. The beam bridges 0.5 mm above the cover back, so the slicer prints it as straight lines along its length. It flexes in the cover plane. The bending load goes along the lines, not across the layers. An upright pin or hook would bend across the layers and crack.
  • Lock. The 0.4 mm bump clicks behind a 45° lip at the pocket mouth. The bump presses 0.05 mm on the lip, which pulls the cover flat and flush.
  • Release. A pull on the cover makes the 45° lip push the bump back. The catch releases and does not break.
  • Location. The posts fit their pockets with 0.15 mm clearance on each side. They locate the cover in X and Z.
  • No support. The baffle prints front face down. The pocket is narrow at the mouth and wider inside, so its walls only step back. The floor is a 3.3 mm bridge.

Membranes and pilot holes

A hole that opens to the bed fills with support, and a horizontal hole sags. The model therefore prints small holes as Ø1.6 pilot holes with a 45° tip, and it closes each counterbore with a 0.25 mm membrane. The slicer prints each membrane as one straight bridge. Drill or cut each one after the print.

10 · Assembly

Six steps

  1. Prepare the sleeve. Remove the supports below the four front posts. Drill the four front post holes Ø4.0 × 7. Install 8 inserts (4 front, 4 rear). Glue the four feet into the recesses on the bottom.
  2. Mount the speakers on the baffle. Drill the membranes. Install 8 inserts in the spacer frames. Put foam tape on the driver flanges. Put each driver on its frame from the back, terminals down. Install four M3 × 5 pan head screws in each driver.
  3. Fit the tray and the amplifier. Drill the tray tabs and install 2 inserts. Hold the tray against the baffle back. Install two M3 × 8 cap screws from the front. Remove the knob and the nut from the ZK-502C. Put the board on the rails, with the volume control and the AUX socket through the baffle. Install the nut in its recess. Install two self-tapping screws (Ø1.9 × 7) through the rear mounting holes into the posts. Stop when the head touches the board. Connect the speaker wires.
  4. Install the front module. Push it into the sleeve front. Install four M3 × 8 cap screws through the baffle corners into the posts.
  5. Install the rear panel. Align the DC socket with its hole. Install four M3 × 6 cap screws.
  6. Fit the covers and the knob. Push the strip on until its four catches click. Push the grille on until its four catches click: the two upper heads go through the grille ports. Push the knob onto the shaft.
To remove the covers. Pull off the knob. Put a flat blade in the slot at the top edge of the strip, below the grille centre. Lift the bottom edge of the grille, then pull the grille off. Then lift the top edge of the strip and pull the strip off.

12 · Open items

Before production

  • The ZK-502C part positions in the model come from photos. Measure the real board and change amp_parts() and the port values in MParams.
  • The volume control shaft is drawn round. Check whether the board has a knurled or a D-shaft, and change the knob bore to match.
  • The snap forces in section 7 are estimates. Confirm them with the test coupons.
  • The Orca profiles do not have the calibrated values yet. Use the Cura profiles.
  • The drawing sheets give the main dimensions. For a size that a sheet does not show, measure the STEP file.
  • The fit of a 3 mm short insert in a printed Ø4.0 hole depends on the insert brand. Test one insert in a coupon.
