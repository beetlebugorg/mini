| 3S protection board (BMS), 10 A, with balance leads and NTC | 1 | Local battery shop, or any 3S BMS from an electronics supplier |

There is no standard 3S1P "stick" pack in this shape. Two options:

1. Buy the cells and the BMS. Ask a local battery or e-bike shop to spot-weld
   the pack in one line and to add a 3 A lead.
2. Order a custom pack from a pack builder. Specify: 3S1P, Molicel P45B,
   cells in one line, BMS at one end, 10 kΩ NTC, JST-XH 2-pin lead, 150 mm.

Do not solder directly to the cells.

## Changes to make before you order

1. **Encoder shaft length.** The model uses a 30 mm shaft. Standard EC11
   encoders have a shaft of 20 mm or less. A 20 mm shaft reaches only 1 mm
   into the knob. Change the knob to a long hub that goes back through the
   strip and the baffle, or move the encoder forward on a small daughter PCB.
2. **LCD module outline.** The model uses a generic 73 × 44 mm carrier with
   holes at ±34 × ±18.5 mm. Choose the module first. Then set
   `lcd_carrier` and `lcd_holes` in `cad/bluespeaker.py` from its drawing.
